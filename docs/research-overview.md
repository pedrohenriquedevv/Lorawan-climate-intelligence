# Research Overview

## Objective

Develop a Data Science pipeline capable of discovering climate patterns from historical meteorological datasets and transforming them into Edge Computing rules for LoRaWAN networks.

## Research Areas

- Data Engineering
- Data Mining
- IoT
- LoRaWAN
- Edge Computing
- Environmental Monitoring

## Data Source

Historical meteorological datasets obtained from INMET stations.

## Expected Outcome

Identification of statistically valid atmospheric patterns capable of supporting autonomous decision-making in IoT devices.
